🚛 Road Freight Transport Cost Calculatorកម្មវិធីគណនាថ្លៃដឹកជញ្ជូនទំនិញផ្លូវគោកសមាមាត្រនឹងការប្រែប្រួលតម្លៃប្រេងឥន្ធនៈA transparent, digital-first, bilingual (Khmer/English) web application designed to calculate road freight transport costs proportionally to fuel price fluctuations.This repository (calculate-shipping-costs-proportionally-to-fuel-price-fluctuations) contains the source code for the interactive calculator, developed in alignment with the Royal Government of Cambodia's Pentagon Strategy for logistics efficiency and digital transformation.📖 Background & ContextThis tool was conceptualized following the pivotal meeting on June 08, 2026, chaired by H.E. Heng Norn, Secretary of State, Ministry of Public Works and Transport (MPWT). The session brought together key stakeholders including the General Department of Logistics, Phnom Penh Autonomous Port, and major private sector associations:CAMTA (Cambodia Motor Truck Association)CLA (Cambodia Logistics Association)LOSCBA (Logistics and Supply Chain Business Association in Cambodia)The Objective: To establish a transparent, effective, and responsive pricing mechanism that adapts to real-world fuel price variations, bridging the gap between public policy and private sector operations.🎯 Alignment with ATZ + Partners VisionAs an initiative driven by the principles of ATZ + Partners, this project strictly adheres to the 7-Tiered Mindset:Intellectual Property (IP): Serves as a proprietary, evidence-based calculation methodology.Digital-First Approach: Transitions manual, opaque negotiations into a transparent, accessible web application.Consulting & Applied Research: Uses a data-driven formula to provide a fair baseline for both transport operators and policy-makers.Community & Partnerships: Fosters trust and collaboration between the Royal Government (MPWT) and the private logistics sector.Sustainability: Encourages efficient resource allocation and resilient supply chain practices.✨ Key Features🌐 Bilingual Interface: Fully supports both Khmer and English, ensuring inclusivity for all local and international stakeholders.📊 Dynamic Transparency Formula: Instantly calculates the adjusted freight cost based on base fuel prices, current fuel prices, and fuel cost ratios.📱 Responsive Design: Accessible across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices (Mobile-First approach for truck drivers and operators).⚡ Real-time Analytics: Displays precise price variances and adjusted costs instantaneously.🧮 The Transparency FormulaThe application utilizes the following applied research formula to determine fair pricing:Adjusted Freight Cost = Base Cost + [Base Cost × Fuel Share Ratio × ((Current Fuel Price - Base Fuel Price) / Base Fuel Price)]
Base Cost: The agreed initial transport fee.Fuel Share Ratio: The percentage of the total operating cost that fuel accounts for (e.g., 40%).Price Variance: The calculated percentage increase or decrease in cost.🚀 Getting StartedTo run this project locally for further development or assessment:PrerequisitesNode.js (v16.0 or higher)npm or yarnInstallationClone the repository:git clone [https://github.com/hengkimleng/calculate-shipping-costs-proportionally-to-fuel-price-fluctuations.git](https://github.com/hengkimleng/calculate-shipping-costs-proportionally-to-fuel-price-fluctuations.git)
